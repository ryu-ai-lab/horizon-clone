## [Analysis Finds Limited AI Productivity Gains Outside Tech Sector](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/insights-news/insights/daily-spark/no-signs-of-ai-payoff-outside-the-tech-sector) ⭐️ 9.0/10

An analysis indicates that despite widespread anticipation, substantial productivity benefits from AI have not yet materialized in industries beyond the technology sector. This observation has sparked discussions regarding factors such as adoption timelines, necessary capital investments, and how AI companies capture value. This finding is significant as it challenges the popular narrative of immediate, widespread AI-driven productivity boosts across all sectors, influencing business investment strategies and public perception of AI's economic impact. It highlights the complex and potentially delayed nature of technological integration and its benefits. The analysis points to several potential reasons for the lack of observed gains, including the time required for industries to adapt and make necessary capital investments, and the possibility that much of the economic value generated by AI is currently being captured by the AI technology providers themselves. Historical parallels, such as the electrification era, are drawn to explain the delayed impact.

## [HTTP2Shell/Broccoli2Shell RCE Vulnerability Affects HTTP Libraries via Brotli](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/python-packages-vulnerable-node2shell-brotli-cve-2026-111497) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A critical remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability, dubbed HTTP2Shell or Broccoli2Shell, has been identified in numerous HTTP libraries due to Brotli compression, raising concerns about its prior misclassification as a non-security issue. This vulnerability is significant as it allows unauthenticated remote code execution across a wide range of HTTP libraries and applications, posing a severe risk to affected systems and highlighting potential gaps in past vulnerability assessments. The vulnerability specifically leverages Brotli compression within HTTP requests, and despite being reported as a dangerous RCE, it was initially not recognized as a security issue, delaying the release of fixes. Some suggest the issue might extend beyond HTTP to any manual Brotli decoding.

hackernews · theanonymousone · 10월24일 08:06 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50219177)

**배경 지식**: Brotli is a lossless data compression algorithm developed by Google, widely used for compressing web content to improve loading speeds. It employs a combination of the LZ77 algorithm, Huffman coding, and 2nd-order context modeling to achieve high compression ratios.

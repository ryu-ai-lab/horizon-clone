## [Critique of Dario Amodei's AI Safety Stance and Open-Source AI Control](https://twitter.com/ylecun/status/2124550037912920090) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A discussion has emerged critiquing Dario Amodei's (Anthropic) AI safety advocacy, suggesting it's a strategic move to restrict open-source AI models and consolidate industry control. This perspective posits that his actions aim to prevent widespread access to advanced AI, thereby benefiting closed-source developers. This debate is highly significant as it directly impacts the future direction of AI development, influencing regulatory frameworks, industry competition, and the balance between innovation and perceived safety. The outcome could determine whether AI technology remains accessible to a broad community or becomes concentrated among a few powerful entities. The critique specifically alleges that Dario Amodei's departure from OpenAI was motivated by a desire for control, and his current AI safety narrative is compared to historical efforts to suppress knowledge. Notably, community discussions include concrete ideas like requiring AMD and Nvidia to implement microcode updates limiting consumer GPU capabilities for LLMs to restrict open-source models.

hackernews · MrBuddyCasino · 11월23일 17:07 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50615308)

**배경 지식**: Dario Amodei is the CEO of Anthropic, an AI safety-focused company, and a former key researcher at OpenAI, known for developing large language models. Open-source AI models, or "open weight models," are those where the trained model weights and often the code are publicly accessible, enabling widespread use and modification. The core tension in this discussion revolves around the perceived safety risks of such accessible models versus the benefits of open innovation.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community discussion is largely critical of Dario Amodei's stance, viewing it as a self-serving attempt to control the AI industry and restrict open-source models for profit. Commenters expressed concerns about suppressing knowledge, with some suggesting that the real danger lies with frontier labs' compute rather than open models. Notably, specific regulatory ideas, such as hardware-level limitations on consumer GPUs, were proposed to curb open-source AI capabilities, though some also offered a nuanced view that AI is not inherently dangerous yet but risks should be taken seriously.

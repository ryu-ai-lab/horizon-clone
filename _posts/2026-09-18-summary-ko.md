## [Wax Motor: A Simple Actuator Utilizing Thermal Expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wax_motor) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The news item explores the 'wax motor,' a mechanical actuator that converts thermal energy into mechanical motion through the thermal expansion of wax. This simple yet effective mechanism is highlighted for its reliability and precision in various applications. Wax motors are significant for their reliability and precision in converting thermal energy into mechanical force, making them ideal for applications requiring accurate control without complex electronics. Their simplicity contributes to their long lifespan and cost-effectiveness in various industrial and consumer products. Wax motors operate by exploiting the substantial expansion of paraffin wax when heated, which can be precisely controlled by selecting waxes with specific melting points to match ambient operating temperatures. Community discussions highlight their use in automotive thermostats for coolant flow control and note the significant volume change of paraffin wax, which can create voids upon solidification.

Thermal expansion is the tendency of matter to change in volume in response to a change in temperature, with most substances expanding when heated and contracting when cooled.

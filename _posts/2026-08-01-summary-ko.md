## [DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 LLM Shows Frontier Intelligence and Value](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 LLM has been released, demonstrating frontier intelligence, high performance, and an exceptional price-performance ratio, making it a strong contender for practical applications. This July 31 update to the Flash model significantly outperforms its preview version, especially in agentic capabilities. This model's combination of advanced capabilities and cost-effectiveness makes sophisticated LLMs more accessible for daily use and local deployment, potentially democratizing advanced AI and reducing operational costs for developers and businesses. Its ability to be run locally addresses privacy concerns and eliminates API costs, fostering innovation in offline and privacy-sensitive applications. DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731, with 284B parameters (13B active), significantly improves agentic capabilities, scoring 82.7 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 54.4 on DeepSWE, and even outperforms the V4-Pro (Preview) model. It is noted for its ability to be run locally, with an Unsloth lossless Q8 version requiring 162GB, offering GLM 5.2/Gemini 3.6 level intelligence for $0.28/m output.

**배경 지식**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI models trained on vast amounts of text data to understand, generate, and process human language, enabling tasks like translation, summarization, and code generation. Local deployment of LLMs refers to running these models directly on a user's personal hardware rather than relying on cloud-based services, offering benefits such as enhanced privacy, reduced costs, and offline functionality.

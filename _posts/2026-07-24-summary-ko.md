## [Startup Founders Urge US Not to Restrict Chinese Open-Weight AI](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/07/22/startup-founders-urge-trump-not-to-shut-off-chinese-open-weight-ai-01008992) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Startup founders are urging the U.S. government to refrain from implementing restrictions on Chinese open-weight AI models, arguing that such a move would negatively impact innovation and competition within the American AI industry. This appeal comes amidst considerations by the U.S. administration to potentially sanction companies involved in the "distillation" of American models. This debate is critical as it could significantly shape the future of AI development, competition, and the startup ecosystem by influencing access to foundational models and fostering or hindering global collaboration. A decision to restrict open-weight AI could have far-reaching geopolitical and economic implications for the entire AI industry. The core concern from the U.S. government side, as mentioned by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, involves investigating whether Chinese AI companies have improperly "distilled" American models, potentially leading to sanctions for intellectual property violations. Startup founders counter that such bans are ineffective against malicious actors and could stifle legitimate innovation, advocating for open data and models to give everyone an advantage.

**배경 지식**: An open-weight AI model refers to an artificial intelligence model whose core components, specifically its parameters or "weights," are publicly released, allowing anyone to download, use, and modify them. Unlike fully open-source AI, open-weight models may not include the training code or datasets, but their accessibility fosters broader innovation and research. This contrasts with proprietary or closed models, where weights are kept private.

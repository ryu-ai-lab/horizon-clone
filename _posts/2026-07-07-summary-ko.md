## [Anthropic Proposes "Global Workspace" Theory for LLM Abstract Reasoning](https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic's new research introduces a "global workspace" theory, suggesting that large language models like Claude possess an internal mechanism for processing and integrating information akin to human conscious access. This theory proposes that LLMs can form verbalizable representations that are broadcast and made available to many internal processes for abstract reasoning. This research is significant as it provides a theoretical framework for understanding the internal workings of LLMs, potentially advancing AI interpretability and shedding light on how these models achieve complex cognitive functions like abstract reasoning. Understanding this mechanism could lead to more robust, controllable, and human-like AI systems. The research tests five functional properties of a global workspace in language models, drawing inspiration from a prominent neuroscience theory of conscious access. Notably, the "J-Space" is defined as the expectation of how much a final logit output would change due to a small change in a particular layer, suggesting an abstract reasoning subspace shared across contexts.

**배경 지식**: The "global workspace theory" in neuroscience posits that conscious experience emerges from a central processing area where information from various specialized brain modules is integrated and broadcast to the entire system, enabling flexible reasoning and decision-making. Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI models trained on massive text datasets, designed to understand, generate, and process human language, often exhibiting emergent capabilities like reasoning.

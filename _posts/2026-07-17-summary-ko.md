## [Kimi K3 LLM Claims Frontier Performance, Plans Open Weights Release](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Kimi K3, a new large language model from a Chinese lab, has been announced, claiming frontier-level performance second only to models like Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, with plans to release full model weights and a technical report. This model also demonstrated an early proof of concept by autonomously designing a chip serving a nano model built on its own architecture within 48 hours. The claim of frontier-level performance combined with the promise of releasing full model weights could significantly accelerate open-source AI research and intensify competition among leading AI developers globally. This move could democratize access to advanced AI capabilities, potentially shifting the landscape of the large language model industry. Kimi K3's evaluations place its overall intelligence second only to Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, demonstrating its capability through a proof of concept where it autonomously designed a chip for a nano model within 48 hours. However, a notable caveat is that Moonshot AI, the developer, may use API content for model training unless specific enterprise agreements are in place.

## [NotebookLM Rebrands to Gemini Notebook](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Google has officially rebranded its AI-powered research and note-taking tool, NotebookLM, to Gemini Notebook. This change aligns the product with Google's broader Gemini AI ecosystem, signaling a strategic move in its AI product lineup. This rebranding is significant as it consolidates Google's AI offerings under the unified Gemini brand, potentially enhancing brand recognition and user adoption for its AI productivity tools. It reflects Google's ongoing effort to integrate its advanced AI capabilities across its product suite. Gemini Notebook functions as an AI-powered research and note-taking assistant, designed to help users interact with their documents and audio content. The rebranding has sparked discussions among users regarding its practical applications, comparisons to other AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude Voice, and Google's overall product strategy.

**배경 지식**: NotebookLM, now Gemini Notebook, is an AI-powered tool developed by Google that allows users to upload documents, articles, and other content to create AI-generated summaries, explanations, and insights. It aims to assist with research, learning, and content comprehension by providing an interactive way to engage with source material.

## [Kagi Adds Paywall Filter to Search Results](https://kagi.com/changelog#11296) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Kagi has introduced a new setting allowing users to filter out paywalled links from their search results, significantly improving the user experience for its paid search service. This feature directly addresses a common frustration of encountering inaccessible content. This is a significant user-centric feature for a paid search engine, as it directly tackles a major pain point for users seeking information without subscription barriers. It reinforces Kagi's value proposition as a premium service focused on quality and user control in an increasingly fragmented web. The new setting enables users to actively remove links that require a subscription or payment to access, preventing frustration and wasted clicks. This feature is particularly valuable for Kagi's paid subscribers who expect a streamlined and efficient information retrieval experience.

**배경 지식**: Kagi is a premium, paid search engine that aggregates results from various sources, including its own indexes, aiming to provide a high-quality, ad-free search experience. It positions itself as an alternative to traditional search engines, addressing issues like "enshittification" and the declining quality of search results often associated with ad-driven models. Users pay a monthly fee for its services, which can include access to AI models.

## [Apple Lays Off Vision Pro and Siri Teams, Signaling Strategic Shifts](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983451/apple-layoffs-vision-pro-siri) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has reportedly laid off over 200 employees across its Vision Pro and Siri teams, specifically impacting the Vision Pro gaming and immersive content development groups. This move includes largely shutting down a Vision Pro gaming team and reducing the size of the immersive content team. These layoffs signal potential strategic shifts or challenges within Apple's AR/VR and AI initiatives, particularly for its high-profile Vision Pro headset and long-standing Siri AI assistant. The reduction in immersive content and gaming teams for Vision Pro could indicate a re-evaluation of its initial content strategy or market focus. The layoffs specifically targeted teams responsible for developing gaming and immersive content for the Vision Pro, with over 200 jobs eliminated across both Vision Pro and Siri divisions. This suggests a potential shift away from certain types of content development for the new spatial computing platform.

**배경 지식**: Apple Vision Pro is a mixed-reality headset running on visionOS, Apple's extended reality operating system, which was unveiled in June 2023 and launched in February 2024. It is designed for spatial computing, allowing users to interact with digital content in their physical space. Siri is Apple's long-standing virtual assistant, powered by artificial intelligence, capable of voice commands, answering questions, and performing tasks.

## [NASA Cancels Mars Sample Return Mission Amid Soaring Costs and Delays](https://www.science.org/content/article/nasa-s-mars-sample-return-mission-dead) ⭐️ 9.0/10

NASA has officially canceled its Mars Sample Return mission, a flagship project aimed at bringing Martian samples to Earth, citing ballooning costs and significant delays that pushed the projected return date to 2040. This cancellation has major implications for planetary science, international space collaboration, and the future direction of space exploration funding, especially as other nations pursue similar ambitious missions. The mission's cost had escalated to an estimated $11 billion, and its design was criticized for relying on legacy rockets like Ariane 64 rather than exploring more cost-effective, higher-capacity options such as Starship or New Glenn.

hackernews · Muhammad523 · 9월21일 19:14 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49791939)

**배경 지식**: The Mars Sample Return mission was an ambitious endeavor to retrieve rock and soil samples collected by NASA's Perseverance rover on Mars, bringing them back to Earth for in-depth scientific study. Such missions are vital for scientists to analyze Martian geology, search for signs of past life, and gather critical data for future human missions to the Red Planet.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community expressed frustration over the cancellation, with some questioning the allocation of tax dollars and others highlighting parallel international programs like China's Mars sample return. Many suggested that newer, cheaper rocket technologies such as Starship could offer more cost-effective approaches, while concerns were also raised about JPL's leadership and the mission's reliance on older rocket technology.

Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for fostering healthier digital environments and promoting intentional technology use. The article highlights a shift from early web browsing, characterized by specific bookmarks and intentional site visits, to current platforms driven by algorithms and endless feeds designed for maximum engagement. This analysis offers crucial lessons for technology companies on avoiding strategic and operational pitfalls, demonstrating how even leading innovators can falter due to poor business execution and product strategy. Key mistakes included a cumbersome sales process that alienated customers, the brief cancellation of Solaris on x86 in 2002 which deterred many users, and a failed partnership deal with Google in 2002 over data sharing demands.

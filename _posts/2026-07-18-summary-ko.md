## [AWS Reports Inaccurate Estimated Billing Data Reaching $1.7 Billion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48945241) ⭐️ 9.0/10

An AWS user reported an erroneous estimated bill of $1.7 billion for the current month, despite their normal usage being less than $5, prompting an urgent support ticket and sparking a large community discussion. This incident highlights critical vulnerabilities in cloud billing systems, which can cause significant financial anxiety and operational disruptions for users, emphasizing the need for robust and accurate billing infrastructure from major cloud providers like AWS. Community members suggest the error is likely due to a unit conversion mistake, such as confusing gigabytes (GB) with bytes, leading to massive overestimations, similar to past incidents where 5¢/GB was misinterpreted as 5¢/byte.

**배경 지식**: Cloud computing services like AWS charge users based on their consumption of resources such as storage, data transfer, and compute time. Billing systems are designed to track this usage and generate invoices, often relying on complex unit measurements and pricing plans.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community discussion reveals a shared sentiment of frustration and disbelief, with multiple users reporting similar experiences of wildly inaccurate estimated bills, often attributed to unit conversion errors (e.g., GB vs. Bytes) in AWS's billing system, and some expressing concerns about AWS's engineering quality.

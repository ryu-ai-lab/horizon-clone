The key distinction of this proof is its focus on the 'unforced' Navier-Stokes equations, which is considered more fundamental and realistic compared to previous demonstrations involving 'forced' versions that used carefully designed external forces.

The Millennium Problems are seven challenging mathematical problems posed by the Clay Mathematics Institute in 2000, with a $1 million prize for the first correct solution to each.

## [Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new research paper introduces a method where a language model can rewrite its own internal weights through reasoning, improving its performance without relying on traditional gradient-based training. The model iteratively tests its self-modifications on practice problems and adopts only the successful changes, leading to improved accuracy over multiple sessions. This research is significant as it represents a novel approach towards creating truly self-improving AI systems that can adapt and enhance their capabilities autonomously, potentially transforming how language models are developed and maintained. It challenges the conventional paradigm of relying solely on external, gradient-based training for model improvement. A key detail highlighted by the community is that the model's self-modification primarily involved reducing the length of its reasoning traces to avoid hitting token limits, rather than fundamental performance improvements. While the paper claims "no gradients and no training," this definition is debated by some, who note the iterative testing and selection of edits resembles a form of training.

hackernews · lwhsiao · 11월20일 16:29 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50577265)

**배경 지식**: Traditionally, language models are trained using gradient descent, where weights are adjusted based on error signals to minimize a loss function. Once deployed, these models are typically static and do not learn further without retraining. The concept of self-modifying code, where a program alters its own instructions, has existed in computer science, but applying this to neural network weights without gradients is a newer area of exploration for AI systems.

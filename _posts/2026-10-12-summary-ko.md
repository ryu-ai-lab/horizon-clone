The author began a personal exploration into running powerful AI models locally on their own hardware, motivated by privacy concerns regarding sending personal data to cloud-based AI services. The author's journey involves testing models like Hermes and Qwen on a Mac Studio, leveraging advancements in quantized AI models and local LLM inference software that enable powerful AI to run efficiently on consumer-grade hardware.

**배경 지식**: Local AI refers to running AI models directly on a user's device, rather than relying on remote cloud servers, which enhances data privacy and reduces latency. Quantization is a technique used to reduce the size and computational requirements of AI models, making them more suitable for local execution on devices with limited resources. This process often involves converting model weights from higher precision (e.g., FP16) to lower precision (e.g., Q2, Q3, Q4) formats, as mentioned in the search results.

## [Moonshot AI Releases 3-Trillion Parameter Kimi-K3 Model on HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Moonshot AI has released its Kimi-K3, a massive 3-trillion parameter language model, on HuggingFace, making its weights available to the public. This move allows developers and organizations to access and potentially customize a highly powerful AI model. This release is significant as it provides a powerful 3-trillion parameter model for broader access, enabling greater customization, performance optimization on specific data, and enhanced IP sovereignty for users. It could democratize access to advanced AI capabilities beyond proprietary APIs. Kimi-K3 is mxfp4 native and would require approximately 1.5TB of VRAM for hosting, suggesting a need for at least 8x B200 GPUs, with 16x B200s recommended for optimal context and throughput. Initial pricing from Fireworks.ai indicates significant costs for inference, with uncached input at $3.00/M and output at $15.00/M.

hackernews · nateb2022 · 7월27일 06:18 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49065752)

**배경 지식**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on vast amounts of text data, with "parameters" referring to the billions or trillions of weights and biases that define the model's complexity and learning capacity. HuggingFace is a popular platform that serves as a central hub for machine learning models, datasets, and tools, enabling researchers and developers to share and collaborate on AI projects.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community discussion highlights significant interest in Kimi-K3's hosting requirements and economic implications, noting the substantial VRAM (1.5TB) and GPU (8-16x B200s) needed, which will establish pricing benchmarks for 3-trillion parameter models. Many emphasize that the true value lies in the model's customization potential and the IP sovereignty it offers to startups, despite concerns about the lack of suitable prosumer hardware for such large LLMs.

## [AI Tool Guide Evolves to Agentic Systems, Shifting from Chat Models](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/27/an-opinionated-guide-to-which-ai-to-use-to-do-stuff/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Simon Willison observed the evolution of Ethan Mollick's AI guide, noting a significant shift from simple chat models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to more powerful agentic systems capable of performing extensive human-like work. The updated guide highlights new agentic modes such as ChatGPT Work/Codex and Claude Cowork/Code, while Gemini has been removed from the latest recommendations. This evolution signifies a crucial trend in AI application, moving beyond basic conversational interfaces to autonomous systems that can execute complex, multi-step tasks, which could revolutionize productivity and user interaction with AI tools. The shift indicates a growing demand for AI that can act as a proactive assistant rather than just a reactive chatbot. The article points out the confusing naming conventions for agentic modes across different platforms, such as ChatGPT Work/Codex and Claude Cowork/Code, which operate differently depending on whether they are accessed via mobile or desktop apps. Specifically, ChatGPT mobile's 'Work' mode now allows its Code Interpreter container to access the internet, offering enhanced capabilities compared to previous restrictions.

**배경 지식**: Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems designed to perform complex tasks autonomously, often involving multiple steps, decision-making, and interaction with various tools or environments, mimicking human-like work processes. Large Language Models (LLMs) are the foundational technology behind these systems, providing the natural language understanding and generation capabilities that enable them to interpret instructions and communicate results.

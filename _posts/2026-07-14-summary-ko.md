## [Sega CD's Silpheed: Art and Engineering of FMV 3D Illusion](https://fabiensanglard.net/silpheed/index.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A technical deep-dive article re-examines Sega CD's "Silpheed," highlighting its innovative use of Full Motion Video (FMV) to simulate a convincing 3D experience on 2D hardware. This analysis is significant as it demonstrates how clever engineering and artistic design overcame severe hardware limitations of the Sega CD, offering valuable insights into retro game development and optimization techniques. Silpheed achieved its 3D-like effect by pre-rendering 3D models into Full Motion Video sequences, then overlaying a 2D sprite for the player's ship, effectively simulating 3D gameplay on 2D-only hardware.

hackernews · ibobev · 7월13일 14:52 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48893639)

**배경 지식**: The Sega CD was an add-on for the Sega Genesis/Mega Drive, notable for its CD-ROM drive that enabled larger game sizes and Full Motion Video (FMV) playback, despite lacking dedicated 3D graphics hardware. FMV games typically relied on playing pre-recorded video sequences, often with interactive choices, to deliver a more cinematic experience than cartridge-based games.

## [Building Mac/iOS Apps Without Xcode Using Command-Line Tools and LLMs](https://scottwillsey.com/building-and-shipping-mac-and-ios-apps-without-ever-opening-xcode/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new approach demonstrates how to build, sign, notarize, and install Mac and iOS applications entirely through command-line tools and AI agents like LLMs, eliminating the direct use of Xcode. This method significantly streamlines Apple app development workflows, enabling greater automation and integration into CI/CD pipelines, and underscores the increasing capability of AI agents in complex software engineering tasks. The core process involves using command-line tools for tasks such as archiving, Developer ID-signing, notarizing, stapling, and local installation, with an LLM often generating or orchestrating the necessary scripts.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models that can generate human-like text and code, increasingly used in software development for automation. AI agents, often powered by LLMs, are designed to perform specific tasks autonomously, streamlining workflows like those found in CI/CD, which automates the building, testing, and deployment of software.

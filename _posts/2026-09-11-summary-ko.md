## [NASA's Mars Imaging Technique Reveals Hidden Earth Rock Art](https://gizmodo.com/this-nasa-color-trick-was-meant-for-mars-now-its-unveiling-rock-art-on-earth-2000809844) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A false-color imaging technique, originally developed by NASA for analyzing the Martian surface, is now being successfully applied to reveal previously unseen ancient rock art on Earth. This method leverages specific spectral bands to enhance features not visible to the naked eye, offering a new tool for archaeological discovery. This application is significant as it provides archaeologists with a powerful, non-invasive method to discover and study ancient human history, potentially leading to breakthroughs in understanding past cultures and their artistic expressions. It also demonstrates the versatility of space technology, finding unexpected utility in terrestrial scientific fields. The technique involves creating false-color composites by assigning different spectral bands (e.g., infrared, visible light) to the red, green, and blue channels of an image, which can highlight subtle differences in material composition or surface features. One community member provided a GIMP tutorial for a similar process using LAB decomposition and contrast maximization on chroma channels.

hackernews · gumby · 9월10일 15:29 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645437)

**배경 지식**: False-color imaging is an image processing technique where colors in an image do not represent the true colors of the object as perceived by the human eye. Instead, specific wavelengths (often outside the visible spectrum, like infrared) are mapped to visible color channels to reveal information that would otherwise be invisible, commonly used in remote sensing for vegetation analysis or geological mapping.

## [Hitachi Launches CO2 Heat Pump Water Heaters with Solar-Friendly Controls](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/07/hitachi-launches-co2-heat-pump-water-heaters-with-solar-friendly-tariff-controls/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Hitachi has launched new CO2 heat pump water heaters featuring controls designed to optimize energy consumption with solar-friendly electricity tariffs. This development aims to improve the grid integration of renewable energy by encouraging hot water heating during periods of high solar generation. This launch represents an important step forward in energy efficiency and smart grid integration, enabling better utilization of renewable energy sources. It helps consumers reduce costs while contributing to grid stability and sustainability by shifting energy demand to match renewable supply. The new water heaters utilize CO2 as a refrigerant, which has a low global warming potential and zero ozone depletion potential, making them an environmentally conscious choice. Their integrated controls are specifically designed to interact with dynamic electricity tariffs, allowing for optimized operation during off-peak or high-solar generation hours.

## [Ted Sanders on LLM Performance and Model Size as Key Factor](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/28/ted-sanders/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Ted Sanders, explaining OpenAI's model naming conventions, stated that while LLM performance is multidimensional, model size is the primary variable explaining variations in performance, reliability, cost, and recall of esoteric facts. He noted that larger models generally offer better performance but come with higher costs per token. This insight is crucial for understanding the fundamental trade-offs in large language model development and deployment, guiding researchers and developers in balancing performance expectations with computational costs. It highlights that despite complex capabilities, scaling model size remains a dominant strategy for improvement across various LLM applications. Sanders specifically noted that larger models like Astra (compared to Luna, Terra, Sol) exhibit superior performance, reliability, and recall of obscure information, while also incurring higher costs per token. He also clarified that model version numbers (e.g., 6.1 vs. 7) indicate release order, relationship to other models, and the magnitude of improvement, not just sequential numbering.

**배경 지식**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on vast amounts of text data, capable of understanding and generating human-like text. Model size typically refers to the number of parameters within the neural network, which directly impacts its capacity to learn and store information. The concept of "multidimensional performance" acknowledges that LLMs excel in different tasks (e.g., math, humor) to varying degrees.

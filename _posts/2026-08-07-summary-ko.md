## [Herdr Joins Y Combinator, Keeps Runtime Open Source with Apache License](https://herdr.dev/blog/herdr-is-joining-y-combinator/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Herdr, a multi-agent coding tool, announced its acceptance into Y Combinator, a prestigious startup accelerator. Alongside this, Herdr committed to keeping its runtime open source and changed its license from AGPL to the more permissive Apache license. This move is significant for the open-source developer tool ecosystem, as it signals a startup's commitment to open source while securing major investment, potentially influencing other AI agent startups. The license change could also broaden adoption by reducing legal complexities for commercial users. Herdr's runtime, which facilitates multi-agent coding, will remain open source, ensuring transparency and community contributions. The shift from AGPL, a strong copyleft license, to Apache, a permissive license, allows for greater flexibility in commercial and proprietary use without requiring derivative works to be open-sourced.

hackernews · collinmanderson · 8월6일 19:14 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201003)

**배경 지식**: The GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL) is a strong copyleft open-source license, meaning that if software under AGPL is used over a network, any modifications or improvements must also be released under the AGPL. In contrast, the Apache License 2.0 is a permissive open-source license that allows users to freely use, modify, and distribute the software, even for commercial purposes, without requiring them to release their modified versions under the same license. Multi-agent systems are computational systems composed of multiple interacting intelligent agents that work together to solve complex problems.

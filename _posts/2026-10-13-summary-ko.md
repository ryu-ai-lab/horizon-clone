## [Hackers Activate Defrosting in European Supermarket Fridges, Exposing Critical Infrastructure Flaws](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/unprecedented-cyberattack-cripples-supermarket-fridges-across-europe-as-hackers-activate-automatic-defrosting-delivery-and-store-prices-also-impacted-by-incident) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Hackers successfully activated the automatic defrosting function in supermarket fridges across Europe, causing significant disruption to the food supply chain and impacting delivery and store prices. This unprecedented cyberattack highlights severe vulnerabilities in industrial control systems and IoT infrastructure. This incident is significant as it demonstrates how cyberattacks can directly impact critical infrastructure, such as the food supply chain, leading to tangible economic losses and public inconvenience. It underscores the urgent need for enhanced security measures in industrial control systems and IoT devices, especially those managing essential services. The attack is suspected to have exploited vulnerabilities in older Danfoss refrigeration controllers, such as the SM800 (End-of-Support 2021) and SC355 (End-of-Support 2016), indicating a reliance on legacy hardware. This incident follows a similar suspected attack on US military bases in August 2026, raising questions about potential connections or inspiration.

## [Repurposing WiFi 7 Router as 320 Msps Software Defined Radio Receiver](https://github.com/h0m3us3r/w1700k-sdr) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A developer successfully repurposed a consumer WiFi 7 router to function as a 320 Msps Software Defined Radio (SDR) receiver, providing a novel and cost-effective method for high-speed radio signal analysis. This project, detailed on GitHub, demonstrates an innovative hardware hacking approach. This development significantly lowers the barrier to entry for advanced radio signal analysis and experimentation, making sophisticated SDR capabilities more accessible to hobbyists and researchers. It highlights the potential for repurposing common consumer electronics for specialized technical applications, fostering innovation in hardware hacking and wireless communication. The repurposed router achieves a high sampling rate of 320 Msps, which is particularly notable for a 5 GHz receiver, offering capabilities typically found in much more expensive dedicated SDR hardware. This project leverages the existing high-performance components within the WiFi 7 router, specifically its chipset, for signal processing.

hackernews · djfergus · 10월12일 12:41 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50055593)

**배경 지식**: Software Defined Radio (SDR) is a radio communication system where components traditionally implemented in analog hardware (like mixers and filters) are instead implemented by software on a computer or embedded system. This flexibility allows a single SDR device to tune almost any signal and support multiple protocols by simply changing the software. The sampling rate in SDR refers to how many samples per second are taken from an analog signal to convert it into a digital signal, directly impacting the bandwidth of the spectrum that can be analyzed.

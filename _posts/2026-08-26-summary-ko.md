The Nitter project, a popular privacy-focused frontend for X (formerly Twitter), has received cease and desist letters, leading its developer to expect all instances to remain down indefinitely. This legal action effectively signals the shutdown of the widely-used open-source project. This event highlights the ongoing tension between large social media platforms and open-source projects that offer alternative access, raising critical questions about platform control, data access, and the viability of privacy-focused alternatives. It impacts many users who relied on Nitter for a tracking-free experience and sets a precedent for similar community-driven initiatives. The project developer, zedeus, stated that they are awaiting legal advice but anticipate all Nitter instances will remain offline for the foreseeable future, indicating a permanent cessation of service. This move by X underscores its aggressive stance against third-party access methods that bypass its official app and tracking mechanisms.

## [Apple Launches New Mac Studio with M5 Max and M5 Ultra for Local AI](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has introduced the new Mac Studio, powered by its latest M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips, with a strong emphasis on its enhanced capabilities for 'Local AI'. The M5 Ultra notably utilizes UltraFusion technology to connect two M5 Max chips, forming a quad-die architecture for unprecedented performance. This release is significant as Apple continues to advance its custom silicon for high-performance computing, particularly with a strong focus on on-device AI processing, which could reshape how professionals and developers approach AI workloads. The emphasis on 'Local AI' aligns with growing industry trends towards enhanced privacy and efficiency by processing data directly on the user's device. The Mac Studio features the M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips, with the M5 Ultra boasting up to 1.2TB/s internal memory bandwidth by combining two M5 Max dies via UltraFusion. While powerful for specific lower-parameter models, some community members question its 'future proof' capabilities for very large language models (LLMs) exceeding 1T parameters without pipeline parallelism.

hackernews · interpol_p · 8월25일 13:03 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433316)

**배경 지식**: 'Local AI' refers to running artificial intelligence models directly on a user's device rather than relying on distant cloud servers, offering benefits like enhanced privacy and faster processing as data doesn't leave the machine. Apple's M-series chips, such as the M5 Max and M5 Ultra, are custom-designed System on a Chip (SoC) solutions that integrate CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine components, optimized for performance and power efficiency. UltraFusion technology is Apple's method of connecting two Max-series chips to effectively double their capabilities, creating a more powerful Ultra-series chip.

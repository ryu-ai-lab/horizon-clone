## [Solar Power Surpasses Nuclear Globally in Electricity Generation](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/29/solar-surpasses-nuclear-power-globally-for-first-time/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

For the first time globally, solar power has generated more electricity than nuclear power, marking a significant milestone in the ongoing energy transition. This achievement was observed from March 2025 through June 2026, with solar and wind individually surpassing nuclear generation in various months, and both doing so simultaneously in March, April, and May 2026. This milestone signifies a major shift in the global energy landscape, demonstrating the rapid growth and increasing dominance of renewable energy sources in meeting global electricity demands. It underscores the potential for a cleaner, more sustainable energy future and impacts global efforts to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. While nuclear power has a substantially higher capacity factor, solar's rapid growth in actual generated terawatt hours has led to this surpassing. Global solar capacity increased by 27% year-on-year to 2,383 GW, significantly outpacing nuclear capacity growth which rose by only 1.5 GW to 399.5 GW, with operating nuclear capacity even declining by 0.5 GW.

hackernews · xbmcuser · 10월17일 14:01 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50131256)

**배경 지식**: Solar power harnesses energy from the sun using photovoltaic panels, converting sunlight directly into electricity, while nuclear power generates electricity through nuclear fission, typically using uranium to heat water and produce steam for turbines. Capacity factor refers to the ratio of the actual energy output over a period to the maximum possible output, indicating how consistently a power plant operates.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community generally views this milestone positively, emphasizing the rapid growth of solar and wind power, with some noting the importance of actual generated output over capacity. There's a shared sentiment that while this is a significant step, further acceleration of clean energy is crucial, not just for electricity but also for industrial feedstocks, alongside regret over nuclear power's stagnation.

## [Dam Removals Restore Fish Populations in Northeastern US](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/dam-removals-are-bringing-fish-back-across-the-northeastern-united-states-striking-down-one-tiny-barrier-at-a-time-180989679/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Dam removals across the northeastern United States are successfully restoring fish populations by eliminating barriers and re-establishing natural habitats. This initiative is significant as it demonstrates a successful approach to environmental conservation and ecological restoration, offering a model for revitalizing aquatic ecosystems. The restoration efforts often involve removing small, obsolete dams that are at the end of their lifespan, which are costly to maintain and pose future failure risks. Additionally, innovative approaches like artificial seasonal flooding are being used to create fish nurseries and support hatchling growth.

hackernews · sohkamyung · 10월16일 23:47 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50126312)

**배경 지식**: Dams are structures built across rivers to impound water, often for purposes like hydropower generation, flood control, or irrigation, but they can fragment river systems and block the migration of fish species to their spawning grounds. Removing these barriers allows fish to access historical habitats, restoring natural river flow and ecological processes.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community discussion largely supports dam removals, highlighting additional successful projects like the Kennebec River dam sales and innovative solutions such as artificial seasonal flooding for fish nurseries. While some express concern about the loss of water storage for future droughts and clean power, others counter that many removed dams are obsolete, costly to maintain, and no longer serve useful functions.

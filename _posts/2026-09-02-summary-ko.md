## [Google Play Blocks AnkiDroid's Open Collective Donation Link](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/issues/21656) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google Play has blocked the popular open-source flashcard app AnkiDroid from including its Open Collective donation link, citing a policy interpretation regarding "tax-exempt donations" and in-app billing. This action has prompted a significant discussion within the open-source community about platform control and monetization. This incident highlights critical issues concerning app store policies, the sustainability of open-source projects, and the extent of platform control over how developers can fund their work. It underscores the challenges open-source projects face in monetizing their efforts while adhering to strict platform guidelines. Google's policy states that Play billing "must not be used in cases where payments include … tax exempt donations," and the core issue revolves around whether donations to a 501(c)(6) organization, which is tax-exempt but not tax-deductible for donors, fall under this restriction. The AnkiDroid project uses Open Collective, which acts as a fiscal host for its 501(c)(6) entity.

hackernews · hexa555 · 9월1일 10:11 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49520022)

**배경 지식**: Open Collective is an open-source crowdfunding and financial management platform that helps grassroots groups and open-source projects transparently collect and manage funds, often through fiscal hosting. A 501(c)(6) organization is a type of tax-exempt non-profit, typically a business league or chamber of commerce, where the organization itself is exempt from federal income tax, but donations made to it are generally not tax-deductible for the donor, unlike donations to a 501(c)(3) charity.

## [Firefox iOS Ad Blocker Introduced with Significant Limitations](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/ad-blocker-on-ios/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Mozilla has introduced an ad blocker for its Firefox browser on iOS, aiming to enhance user experience and privacy. This new feature is being rolled out experimentally to users. This feature is significant for improving user privacy and browsing speed on mobile, potentially influencing user choice in the competitive iOS browser market. However, its current limitations may hinder its overall impact and adoption. The ad blocker currently does not block ads on search engine results pages or YouTube, requires users to enable telemetry for use, and is undergoing a slow, experimental rollout, leading to user frustration. Its non-general availability has also been a point of contention among the community.

This process monitors system behavior, operational workflows, and performance, often structuring data as metrics, events, logs, and traces. Developers use this data to understand how their applications are being used and to identify potential issues or areas for improvement.

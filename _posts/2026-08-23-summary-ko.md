## [Linus Torvalds Praises AI for Debugging Grunt Work, Notes Its Lack of Persistence](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/22/linus-torvalds/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Linus Torvalds, the creator of Linux, recently revealed he used an AI to significantly assist in a "debug session from hell" for a drm/xe issue, acknowledging its help with "grunt-work" and even allowing the AI to write the final commit message. This endorsement from a highly influential figure like Torvalds validates the practical utility of AI in complex software development and low-level system debugging, while also providing a realistic assessment of its current limitations compared to human problem-solving tenacity. Torvalds highlighted that the AI was "enormously helped" for repetitive tasks like adding and analyzing debug code, but critically noted that it "several times stated flat out that this was impossible and unsolvable," contrasting with his own stubbornness to persevere. The specific issue involved drm/xe: Don't hand out the flat CCS storage as usable VRAM.

Intel Xe graphics driver refers to a new, experimental kernel graphics driver developed by Intel for its integrated and discrete graphics hardware, which is still under active development.

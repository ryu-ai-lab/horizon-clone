## [Navier-Stokes Controversy: Buckmaster Accuses OpenAI of Misappropriation](https://cims.nyu.edu/~tristanb/statement.pdf) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alpöge claim significant progress on Navier-Stokes related problems, specifically regarding finite-time blowup for certain fluid equations, leading to a major academic controversy involving accusations against OpenAI for potentially using their insights. This controversy highlights critical ethical questions about the role of AI in scientific discovery, intellectual property, and academic integrity, impacting the future of collaborative research between human mathematicians and AI systems. Buckmaster and Alpöge's work does not solve the $1,000,000 Millennium Prize problem directly but claims a proof for a similar non-Millennium Navier-Stokes problem, with the controversy escalating due to OpenAI's alleged attempts to offer a 'Clay Prize' acknowledgement and subsequent threats.

hackernews · procedurecall · 9월8일 05:42 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49605915)

**배경 지식**: The Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem is one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems posed by the Clay Mathematics Institute, offering a US$1,000,000 prize for its solution. It concerns the mathematical properties of solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations, which describe fluid motion, and proving whether smooth solutions always exist or if they can break down. Understanding these equations is considered crucial for comprehending turbulence, a major unsolved problem in physics.

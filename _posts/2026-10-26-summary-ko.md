## [Oregon Police Demand $2.5M for Public Records on One Officer](https://theintercept.com/2026/10/01/oregon-police-misconduct-public-records-cost-fee/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The Eugene Police Department in Oregon is demanding an unprecedented $2.5 million fee from a local news outlet for public records concerning a single police officer, claiming it would require 44,816 staff hours to process the request. This exorbitant fee poses a severe threat to government transparency and investigative journalism, potentially setting a dangerous precedent that could effectively block public access to critical information. The police department justified the $2.5 million fee by estimating 44,816 hours of staff time, equivalent to approximately 21 years of work, to fulfill the request for records on a single officer.

## [10-Year-Old CNN Outperforms Frontier LLMs for Skin Cancer Diagnosis](https://lynchlab.net/llm-skin-cancer) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A recent study demonstrated that a decade-old, fine-tuned Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) significantly outperformed frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) in accurately diagnosing skin cancer. This finding highlights the continued superior performance of specialized AI models for specific, critical medical applications. This is significant because it challenges the prevailing notion that frontier LLMs are universally superior, underscoring the critical importance of specialized models for high-stakes tasks like medical diagnosis. It suggests that for specific, critical applications, investing in and refining domain-specific architectures remains crucial for optimal accuracy and reliability. The study utilized Inception v3, a CNN architecture, which was initially pre-trained on everyday photographs and then fine-tuned on 68,011 dermoscopic and clinical images from the ISIC Archive using an H100 GPU. Crucially, 200 test lesions and all related patient data were meticulously excluded from the training and validation sets to ensure an unbiased evaluation.

hackernews · sungam · 10월25일 17:15 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50233779)

**배경 지식**: A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) is a type of deep learning neural network specifically designed to process image data by capturing spatial relationships between pixels, making it highly effective for computer vision tasks. Fine-tuning is a machine learning technique where a pre-trained model, already trained on a large, general dataset, is adapted and further trained on a smaller, specific dataset to optimize its performance for a particular task.

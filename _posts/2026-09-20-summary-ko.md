Google is reportedly making new Android 17 APIs exclusive to Pixel devices, withholding them from the Android Open Source Project (AOSP) for the first time since Android 3.x. This move signals a significant shift towards a more closed Android ecosystem, impacting custom ROMs and other Android distributions. This decision has major implications for the open-source Android ecosystem, custom ROMs, and device manufacturers, as it could limit their ability to implement the latest Android features. It represents a significant step by Google to exert greater control over the Android platform and differentiate its Pixel devices. The new APIs are being introduced in a Pixel-only update, meaning Pixel-exclusive app features will rely on an SDK version that is not available to the broader AOSP community or other OEMs. This creates a divergence where official Android source code updates for OEMs and the public will lack these specific new functionalities.

## [New Brain Progenitors Identified, Improving Brain Stem Cell Cultivation](https://med.stanford.edu/news/all-news/2026/09/two-separate-brains.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers have identified two parallel neural ectoderm progenitors contributing to brain development, specifically anterior and posterior types, which has led to a new and effective technique for growing brain stem cells in vitro. This significant discovery, published in Nature Neuroscience, promises to accelerate neurological disease research. This discovery is significant because it provides a more accurate understanding of early brain development and, more importantly, offers an improved method for culturing brain stem cells, which was previously challenging. This advancement will accelerate research into complex neurological diseases like ALS by providing better experimental models. The research identified two distinct progenitors: the anterior neural ectoderm, responsible for the forebrain and midbrain, and the posterior neural ectoderm, contributing to the hindbrain, both emerging simultaneously during gastrulation. A key practical outcome is the improved ability to grow brain stem cells, especially hindbrain cells, in vitro, which was previously a significant challenge.

hackernews · emigre · 9월19일 05:48 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49763697)

**배경 지식**: Neural ectoderm progenitors are specialized cells that originate from the embryonic ectoderm during early development and are fundamental for forming the entire nervous system. These cells undergo a process called neurulation, where the ectoderm patterns into various lineage progenitors, ultimately giving rise to the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. Understanding these progenitors is key to comprehending how different brain regions diversify and develop.

## [Discovery Loop Aims to Automate Scientific and ML Research](https://www.discoveryloop.com/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Discovery Loop is a new initiative launched to automate the experimental loop across various scientific and engineering disciplines, starting with a focus on machine learning research. This project is described as a large-scale, institutionalized version of concepts like Andrej Karpathy's `autoresearch`. This initiative is significant because it aims to fundamentally transform scientific and engineering research by automating the experimental process, potentially accelerating discoveries and breakthroughs across various fields. Its success could set a new paradigm for how AI and large-scale systems contribute to scientific methodology. The initiative plans to initially focus on ML research and engineering, requiring strong expertise in both machine learning and large-scale systems, and is envisioned as a massively scaled, institutionalized version of Andrej Karpathy's `autoresearch`. Karpathy's original `autoresearch` concept involves AI agents running experiments on a single GPU, with a future vision for asynchronously collaborative agents.

**배경 지식**: The "experimental loop" in science and engineering refers to the iterative process of forming hypotheses, designing and conducting experiments, analyzing results, and using those findings to refine or generate new hypotheses. Andrej Karpathy's `autoresearch` is a notable project that demonstrates how AI agents can automate parts of this loop, specifically in machine learning, by autonomously running and evaluating experiments.

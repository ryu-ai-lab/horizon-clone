## [K2 Horizon Launches Six Open-Source AI Models with Mixed Community Reception](https://ifm.ai/blog/k2/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

K2 Horizon has released a fleet of six new open-source AI models, but initial community feedback indicates significant performance issues and practical application challenges compared to established competitors. The introduction of new open-source AI models is vital for fostering transparency and innovation in the AI/ML community, potentially reducing reliance on closed systems and their inherent risks. K2 Horizon's models are presented as "fully open," encompassing source code and training data details, yet community benchmarks reveal the 32B model underperforms against Qwen3.8 27B, and the 3.7B model exhibits significant hallucination and incorrect code generation in practical tests.

hackernews · karimf · 9월3일 15:36 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49551760)

**배경 지식**: Open-source AI models provide transparency by making their code, weights, and training data public, contrasting with closed models where these details are proprietary. A common issue in large language models is "hallucination," where the AI generates confident but factually incorrect or fabricated information.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community largely supports the move towards fully open AI models, emphasizing transparency in training data and methodology. However, there is significant criticism regarding the K2 Horizon models' performance, with users noting that self-reported benchmarks don't match reality and practical tests reveal issues like hallucination and incorrect code generation compared to established models.

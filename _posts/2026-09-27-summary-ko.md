## [Apple Cards' Origin Story and Impact on Competitors](https://lexontech.org/fifteen-years-later-the-apple-cards-origin-story) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The article revisits the origin story of Apple Cards, a service launched fifteen years ago, highlighting Apple's meticulous design process and unique logistical challenges, such as implementing invisible barcodes for tracking. It also details the significant impact this product had on smaller competitors in the personalized printed card market. This historical account offers insights into Apple's product development philosophy, demonstrating how its entry into a niche market with a highly polished product could disrupt smaller innovators and set new industry standards for user experience and design. It underscores the challenges faced by startups when a tech giant enters their space. Apple insisted on invisible barcodes for envelopes, which required collaboration with a printing company and the USPS to develop and scan them under UV light for tracking without altering the envelope's appearance. This meticulous attention to detail extended to design elements like debossing, which mimicked traditional letterpress printing.

**배경 지식**: Apple Cards was a mobile app and service launched by Apple Inc. that allowed users to create and send personalized physical greeting cards directly from their iPhone, which were then printed and mailed by Apple. This service aimed to provide a seamless, integrated experience for sending tangible mementos in an increasingly digital world.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community discussion reveals that smaller competitors felt "Sherlocked" by Apple Cards, perceiving Apple as using its influence to co-opt their ideas, while also highlighting Apple's extreme attention to detail, such as invisible barcodes. Some users praised the frictionless experience, though one comment also critiqued the "founder-led" company dynamic.

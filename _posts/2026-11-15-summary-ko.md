This incident highlights critical issues in application security, emphasizing the need for robust Row-Level Security and prompting a broader discussion about platform security defaults and developer accountability in preventing data breaches. The core issue was insufficient Row-Level Security, with community discussion pointing to potential culpability of platform defaults, specifically mentioning Supabase, where a fix for existing projects might not have been universally applied.

## [Google Research Highlights Antigravity's /teamwork for Multi-Agent AI Collaboration](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Google Research has showcased its 'Antigravity /teamwork' feature, demonstrating how its multi-agent AI collaboration capabilities can significantly advance complex tasks in science, engineering, and coding. This update highlights recent progress and examples of its application, such as proving a complex information theory conjecture. This feature is significant as it demonstrates the potential of multi-agent AI systems to tackle problems that are difficult or impossible for individual AI agents, accelerating research and development across science, engineering, and software. It could transform how complex projects are approached, enabling faster breakthroughs and more efficient problem-solving. The `/teamwork` command in Google Antigravity deploys collaborative multi-agent teams specifically designed for large software projects, complex simulations, and deep research tasks, where AI agents can critique each other's proposals and work for extended durations. Notably, it was used to fully prove information theory's 2013 Courtade–Kumar conjecture, showcasing its advanced problem-solving capabilities.

These systems coordinate using AI capabilities, with advancements in large language models (LLMs) enabling more sophisticated interactions and collaboration among agents. Unlike agent-based models focused on collective behavior, MAS aims to solve specific practical or engineering problems.

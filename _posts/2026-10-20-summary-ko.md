## [Animal Testing Alternatives Gain Traction Amid Ethical and Efficacy Concerns](https://spectrum.ieee.org/alternatives-to-animal-testing) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The adoption of alternatives to animal testing is increasing, driven by ethical concerns and the potential for more human-relevant and effective research models, with advancements like virtual cell technology showing particular promise. This shift is significant for biomedical research, promising more accurate drug development, reduced animal suffering, and a resolution to long-standing ethical concerns in scientific practice. Key challenges include the reliance on animal-derived components like Matrigel for growing human organoids, yet promising technical advancements are emerging, such as DeepMind's 'virtual cell' research utilizing AI/ML for more sophisticated models.

hackernews · rbanffy · 10월19일 11:10 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50150927)

**배경 지식**: Animal testing has historically been a standard practice in scientific and medical research for evaluating drug safety and efficacy, but it increasingly faces ethical scrutiny regarding animal welfare and the direct relevance of animal models to human biology. Alternatives seek to mimic human physiological responses through methods like in vitro cultures, computational simulations, and organoids.

**커뮤니티 토론**: The community largely supports the move towards alternatives, citing ethical improvements and the potential for more human-relevant research, although some commenters point out that current alternatives like organoids still rely on animal-derived components such as Matrigel. There's also a discussion about the potential for increased overall testing demand (Jevons Paradox) and significant interest in DeepMind's 'virtual cell' research.

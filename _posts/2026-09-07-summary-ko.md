## [Privacy-Focused Service Autistici/Inventati Shuts Down Due to Government Pressure](https://keepitfree.ai/announcements/a/i-shuts-down-stay-human/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Autistici/Inventati (A/I), a long-standing privacy-focused online service, announced its shutdown in September 2026, citing legal and financial risks stemming from its designation as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist by the United States government in August 2026. This decision followed allegations that its services were used to facilitate violent attacks by extremist groups. This shutdown is highly significant as it raises critical concerns about digital rights, internet freedom, and the viability of secure communication tools, especially when platforms face government pressure over user activities. It highlights the challenges independent online services encounter in balancing user privacy with regulatory demands and the potential for censorship. A/I was an Italian hacktivist group providing communication services for left-wing activists, founded in 2001, and had previously faced legal challenges and server seizures. The US government alleged that A/I's tools facilitated attacks by anarchist cells, leading to its designation and subsequent domain unreachability and a compromise of its blogging platform, NoBlogs.

It specifically raises concerns about the potential degradation of personal thought processes and the loss of an authentic human voice in written communication. This discussion is significant as it addresses the profound intellectual and ethical challenges posed by the increasing integration of LLMs into writing, impacting individuals, technical communication, and the broader AI/ML and software engineering communities. It highlights the critical need to consider the long-term effects on human thought and authentic expression. The article's central premise, conveyed through its title "Your intellectual fly is open," metaphorically warns against intellectual vulnerability and oversight when delegating writing to LLMs. It emphasizes that while LLMs can generate text, they lack personal voice and the critical thinking process inherent in human writing.

## [Asahi Linux Officially Supports Apple M3 Macs](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Asahi Linux has officially announced support for Apple's M3 series Macs, marking a significant milestone in bringing full Linux compatibility to the latest Apple Silicon hardware. This is a major technical breakthrough, expanding Linux compatibility to cutting-edge proprietary hardware through complex reverse engineering, which opens new possibilities for users seeking to run Linux on powerful Apple hardware. While official support for M3 is a significant step, community discussions highlight current limitations such as poor `llama.cpp` performance compared to Apple's Metal backend and the lack of full sleep and HDMI support. The project continues to rely on extensive reverse engineering of Apple's proprietary hardware.

hackernews · mdp2021 · 9월6일 14:08 · [커뮤니티 토론](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**배경 지식**: Asahi Linux is a community project dedicated to porting the Linux kernel and related software to Apple Silicon-powered Macs, which requires extensive reverse engineering due to the lack of official documentation from Apple. This effort aims to provide a fully functional Linux environment on Apple's proprietary hardware, starting with the M1 series.
